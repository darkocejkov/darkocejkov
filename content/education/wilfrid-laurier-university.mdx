Faculty of Science, Waterloo, Ontario. Graduated with a GPA of 8.41 / 12
overall — 9.8 in Computer Science, 8.6 in Psychology.

The stream is the part worth explaining. It is a joint honours degree, so the
computer science is the whole thing — data structures, algorithms, theoretical
computation, object-oriented programming, systems work in C — while the
psychology half is pointed deliberately at perception and neuroscience rather
than at the social side. The two get taught against each other: the same
modelling idea shows up as machine learning in one course and as a theory of
how a brain might do it in the next.

Coursework and projects included k-nearest neighbours and k-means implemented
from scratch, pathfinding and tree traversal (BFS, DFS, A\*, greedy), a Google
results scraper piped into ML classification, custom Unix command-line programs
in C, regular expressions and finite automata, recurrence relations and time
complexity, and research papers and proposals on perception and neuroscience.
