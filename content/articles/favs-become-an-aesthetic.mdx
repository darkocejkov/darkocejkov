the word "aesthetic" has been intensely overused. everything has a "core" now. it's escaped containment, and the normies
are describing their things as "fallcore", or "y2kcore". 

this ties into the age of instagram, and how i'm starting to develop my own complex and insecurities because of the 
attitude surrounding aesthetics. fitting into any group is challenging but there's something so sharp about watching
people online make their aesthetic, handcraft it, and you are just existing as a starter pack version of it.
instagram is FOR judging based on the cover. what you look like, how you present yourself.

it's actually crazy to admit but being 28 and reading these comments (likely from teenagers) are getting to me because i 
have a hard time justifying my "place" or "reason" in liking the things i do.
the co-opting of "larping" is a reflection that instagram is the panopticon, you cannot show ANYTHING without being judged on it.
they are always watching and writing their observations in the comments. 
i have started to answer to that voice by coming up with answers to justify everything i do. 
"holy aphex twin larp" is a real thought i had, and after shaking off the cringe i felt like i seriously needed to justify
that i'm not a poser. 

how can i recover when my niche interests are perfectly summarized in a "starter pack". laugh it off, sure.

comparison is the death of joy. to be cringe is to be free (a snowclone, i know...)
