- ai has the power to upset greedy people, because now everyone has the power to make any service just by prompting
- if you build a tool or an app, monetizing it has to be a real choice. if you paywall a service that doesn't actually cost any money - you're greedy; someone will make a better version for free.
- contribute to good, not to the powers that don't do good
