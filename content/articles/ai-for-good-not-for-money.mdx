now everyone has the power to make anything just by prompting without really understanding.

unfortunately, this means that everyone who has a $20 subscription to claude or copilot can create their own versions of
projects and tools that are unfairly monetized.
unfortunately, this means that everyone who has a $20 subscription to claude or copilot WILL be releasing their projects
on github, flooding the pool.

if you build a tool or an app, monetizing it has to be a real choice. 
if you make a software as a service - you're greedy. 
if you paywall a service that doesn't actually cost any money to maintain - you're greedy; someone will make a better version for free.

contribute to good, not to the powers that don't do good
