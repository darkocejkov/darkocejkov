Cutting the ring generator down to four parameters removed a decision I had
been avoiding. The uncomfortable generalisation is that most controls are like
that: a question the design refused to answer, handed instead to whoever opens
the panel next. Usually me, a month later, with less context than I have now.

A knob is cheap to add and expensive to keep. It multiplies the states the
piece can be in, and every one of them has to look deliberate, because someone
will find it.

Where this stops I genuinely do not know yet. Radius and count are the work —
turning them *is* the piece. Others are indecision with a label on it, and from
the inside, on the day I add them, the two look identical.
