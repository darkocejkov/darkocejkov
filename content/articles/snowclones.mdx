snowclones are [defined](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snowclone) as a phrasal template that you can substitute words in.
my mostest favoritest is the dr strangelove title. "dr strangelove: or how i learnt to stop worrying and love the bomb" becomes "X: or how i learnt to stop Y and love the Z".
it's cute for a bit, it's a nice reference. snowclones aren't bad, i'm no linguist or writer, im just an idiot with a keyboard.
just like memes, they have a lifetime. once a meme ends up on facebook, it's dead. once a youtuber uses a dr strangelove clone, it's dead, officially.
it's just so boring and lazy. i've not seen dr strangelove, but i've sure as hell read that name enough times that i
don't really want to anymore.

here's a [blog](https://snowclones.org/the-queue/) that catalogs many of them. and it's quite interesting to know
that you can really make it formulaic, and write the joke into the formula. like `X, and by X I mean [Not X]`. 
that itself is actually kind of funny because you've baked in the subversion of expectations.

anyway, it just sucks to see the laziest uses of language by relying on boring, old, and unfunny references. 
if there's more than 1 hackernews article with that name, don't use it.
